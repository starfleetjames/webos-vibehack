Weather observations: NOAA / National Weather Service
API documentation: https://www.weather.gov/documentation/services-web-api
Public-domain data policy: https://www.weather.gov/disclaimer

Weather is disabled until a station is chosen in local configuration.
The launcher converts observations to Fahrenheit, rounds readings, and maps
condition codes to short descriptions and original SVG icons. This is not an
official NOAA/NWS product and does not imply NOAA/NWS endorsement.

Readings refresh every 15 minutes. Airport reports are normally hourly; readings
older than 75 minutes display their observation time. Readings older than
90 minutes are not shown. Missing readings fall back to a recent valid report.
